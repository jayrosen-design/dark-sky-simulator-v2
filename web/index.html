Gainesville & Alachua County, Florida

Planning simulators

Three map-based tools built on one platform and the same public data: the night sky a lighting policy would make, where roadside wildlife detection would prevent the most crashes, and what modernizing the City's public-art ordinance would fund, protect and reach.

Dark Sky Simulator v2

See the night sky your lighting policy makes.

  • Skyglow model for Alachua, Levy and six neighboring counties
  • Ordinance presets and a fixture-by-fixture Build mode, with costs and payback
  • 3D streets and light sources, and an all-sky view of the stars from any site
  • Observatory siting with land prices, stargazing forecasts and a legislative PDF brief

Uncalibrated planning projections from public data.

WildSight

It sees the deer before the driver does.

  • 3D simulation of a solar roadside unit that detects wildlife and warns drivers
  • The unit's parts up close, a hands-on simulation lab and the team's research
  • Every road in eight counties ranked by expected animal-vehicle crashes
  • Units, LoRa gateways, cost and crashes avoided for any deployment

Simulations are illustrative; planner figures are planning estimates.

Public Art Policy Simulator

What modernizing Chapter 5.5 would fund, protect and reach.

  • The public art collection on a 3D map, lit by the sun for any date and hour
  • The 1989 code against the Trust's discussion draft: caps, CPI indexing, a 15% conservation reserve
  • Daily impressions from traffic and foot traffic, walk access and East Gainesville equity
  • Arts grants and calls to artists across the US, visitor spending, staff-study scenarios and a PDF brief
Open the simulator

Art registry hand-compiled and from the Public Art Archive; an illustrative capital program; planning estimates, not adopted policy.

One platform. The three apps share a mapping core (MapLibre on an OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles dark basemap with OpenStreetMap data), sun and sky astronomy, 3D terrain and buildings, and an offline pipeline over public data: U.S. Census, FDOT traffic counts, Florida parcel rolls, City of Gainesville and Alachua County open data. Every figure is labeled as a planning estimate and links to its sources.
